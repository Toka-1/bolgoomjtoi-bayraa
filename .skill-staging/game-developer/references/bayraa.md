# Bayraa project preferences

- Audience: young children, including children who cannot read. Preserve Mongolian content.
- Rounds start immediately. Keep practice as a separate selectable mode.
- During play, highlight the important object or safe destination. Do not add written round instructions, directional arrows, or moving guidance trails.
- After failure, show the animated safe-action explanation with replay and continue controls. Text quizzes belong to teacher mode.
- Preserve touch/left dragging and desktop right-click pickup/drop. Enter and Space must not select objects; window interactions require clicking the window.
- Smoke/carbon-monoxide scenes teach leaving through the door. River scenes teach staying out of the river, with a distinct adult caregiver and no-swimming sign.
- Outlet failure follows insertion of an object's tip into the socket, not merely picking up or touching the object. Baby/cupboard scenes should visibly hide hazards when the cupboard closes.
- Recheck avatar proportions and reachable surfaces when adding scenes. Adult caregivers should look distinct from the child avatar.

Inspect current source before assuming file names, level counts, or available mechanics; those can change.
